Vision & Mission

01 / Vision

Vision

To be the trusted technology and engineering partner that industries across Indonesia and Southeast Asia rely on for long-term growth.

02 / Mission

Mission

  • 1

    Deliver reliable technology and engineering solutions through leading global partnerships and our own product brands.

  • 2

    Support customers throughout the full lifecycle of every system — from specification and installation to training, spare parts and after-sales service.

  • 3

    Continuously improve our capabilities while operating responsibly toward our customers, employees and the environment.

Our journey

Hanindo Group Growth

  1. 2002

    Hanindo Group established

    Citra, Automotive, Automation Solutions and Flowtech formed within months.

  2. 2005

    CUSTOM partnership

    Partnered to expand CUSTOM products to the China market.

  3. 2007

    China expansion

    Hanindo (Shanghai) International Co., Ltd. office opens, Shenzhen follows in 2009.

  4. 2012

    South East Asia expansion

    PT. Hanindo Automation Solutions adds CUSTOM Hanindo Indonesia.

  5. 2014

    Singapore office

    Gralessando (S) Pte. Ltd. opens, covering CUSTOM Hanindo South East Asia.

  6. 2016–2019

    Regional partnerships

    CUSTOM Hanindo partnerships established in Vietnam, Thailand and the Philippines.

  7. 2023

    Manila office

    A CUSTOM Hanindo office follows the Philippines partnership.

  8. 2026

    Fire Fighting Department

    PT. Hanindo Citra adds fire protection.
