About Us

Our journey

Hanindo Group Growth

  1. 1987

    Where it began

    Petroleum equipment at PT. Sugiron Citra.

  2. 2002

    Hanindo Group established

    Citra, Automotive, Automation Solutions and Flowtech formed within months.

  3. 2005

    CUSTOM partnership

    Partnered to expand CUSTOM products to the China market.

  4. 2007

    China expansion

    Hanindo Shanghai office opens, Shenzhen follows in 2009.

  5. 2012

    South East Asia expansion

    Hanindo Automation Solutions adds CUSTOM Hanindo Indonesia.

  6. 2014

    Singapore office

    Gralessando Pte Ltd opens, covering CUSTOM Hanindo South East Asia.

  7. 2016–2019

    Regional partnerships

    CUSTOM Hanindo partnerships established in Vietnam, Thailand and the Philippines.

  8. 2023

    Manila office

    A CUSTOM Hanindo office follows the Philippines partnership.

  9. 2026

    Fire Fighting Department

    Hanindo Citra adds fire protection.
