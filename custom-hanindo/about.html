About Us

Company overview

From a Shanghai Office
to a Network Across Asia

CUSTOM Hanindo is the Hanindo Group’s dedicated CUSTOM business, built on a partnership with CUSTOM Italy that began in 2005. In 2007, Hanindo (Shanghai) International Co., Ltd. opened in Shanghai, marking the Group’s first operation outside Indonesia, followed by a Shenzhen office in 2009.

In 2012, the CUSTOM product range was introduced in Indonesia through PT. Hanindo Automation Solutions, one of the Hanindo Group’s four founding companies, established in 2002 to serve the oil and gas automation sector. In 2014, Gralessando (S) Pte. Ltd. was established in Singapore as the hub for Southeast Asia. The network subsequently expanded through partnerships in Vietnam, Thailand and the Philippines, with a Manila office opening in 2023.
