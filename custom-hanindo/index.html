Printing, scanning & point of sale across Asia

The Hanindo Group’s CUSTOM business supplies, integrates and supports printing, scanning and point-of-sale hardware for retail, transport and service operators in China and Southeast Asia.

CUSTOM Hanindo

One CUSTOM partner, from China to Southeast Asia.

Since 2005, Hanindo has brought CUSTOM’s range to Asia through its own registered companies rather than agents: in China since 2007, Indonesia since 2012 and Singapore since 2014, with offices and partners now covering the rest of Southeast Asia.

  • Point of sale & data captureTerminals, touch systems, cash registers, scanners and payment terminals.
