NOTE on the training row. The old page offered training on Gilbarco
Veeder-Root equipment and on thermal printers, and nothing on the new
site replaces it. There is no /training/ page, and the only training
content anywhere is the automotive spooring and balancing courses on
/automotive/products-services.html — a different product line, so
sending a Gilbarco enquiry there would be wrong.

It goes to /products-services/ because the old URL spans two product
families, petroleum equipment and printers, and that is the one page
covering both. A visitor arriving from an old search result lands on
what the group actually supplies, with a route to the right company,
rather than on a 404 or the bare home page.

This is the honest fix, not the ideal one. The ideal one is a /training/
page, which needs the business to say what training is still offered —
inventing course details would be worse than the redirect.
