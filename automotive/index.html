PT. Hanindo Automotive

The workshop, fully equipped.

Hanindo Automotive equips and supports professional workshops with complete service bay solutions — from tyre changers, wheel balancers and 3D wheel alignment systems to vehicle lifts, diagnostics, fluid handling, lubrication systems and welding equipment. Every solution is backed by our engineering expertise, installation support and after-sales service.

  • Sole distributor in Indonesia for Airtec, HPA-Faip and RAASM
  • Own brands WELDANN and ATOM, supported by dedicated spare parts and warranty service
  • Installation, commissioning and operator training by our own technicians
  • RAASM fluid handling solutions supplied beyond workshops to lubricant distributors, industrial plants and mining operations
