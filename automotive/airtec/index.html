Tyre Inflation

Digital inflators for the workshop, forecourt, depot and mine.

Back to our other products
AIRTEC CORPORATION

Airtec Corporation builds digital tyre inflation equipment in Singapore, to ISO 9001:2015 certified by SGS. An automatic inflator takes a target pressure and holds a tyre to it — no gauge, no operator standing over it. The range runs from a compact unit that mounts on the tyre changer itself to weather-sealed wall models, high-flow and high-pressure units for fleet and mine work, and vandal-resistant pedestals for a forecourt.

The range

Digital tyre inflators.

Every one reads to the same accuracy. They differ by where they mount, how much air they move, how high they go and who works them.
