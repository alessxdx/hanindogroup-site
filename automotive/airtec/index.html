Airtec

Digital tyre inflation, built in Singapore. Wall-mounted, machine-mounted and high-flow inflators for the service bay, the forecourt, the hangar and the mine.

AIRTEC CORPORATION
Sole distributor for Indonesia

Set the pressure, walk away.

Airtec Corporation designs and builds digital inflation equipment at its head office in Singapore, to ISO 9001:2015 certified by SGS. Hanindo Automotive is its sole distributor in Indonesia.

An automatic inflator takes a target pressure and holds a tyre to it — no gauge, no guesswork, no operator standing over it. Every model reads to ±0.5% of full scale and covers 5 to 145 psi, so one specification suits a passenger car, a truck and a piece of plant.

  • Accurate to ±0.5% of full scale across the range, 5–145 psi / 0.3–10.0 bar
  • IP66 weather-sealed enclosures on the wall-mounted models — indoor or outdoor
  • Manufactured to ISO 9001:2015, accredited by SGS International Certification Services
