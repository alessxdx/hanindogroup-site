Fluid Handling

Hose reels, pumps, dispensing and centralised lubrication.

Tyre Service Tyre Inflation Lifts & Handling Service Equipment Fluid Handling Welding
RAASM

RAASM builds fluid handling equipment in Vicenza, Italy — hose and cable reels, pneumatic and diaphragm pumps, oil and grease dispensing, waste oil handling, diesel equipment and centralised lubrication. Its FLS and FCS systems carry that further, monitoring what is left in every drum and tank and recording who drew what from it. Single units, or a whole lubrication system specified together.
