HPA-Faip — Helper Devices

One model — a universal helper arm that fits the tyre changers above.

Photo to follow
HPA-Faip

Universal Helper Device — ALLY 30

Specifications
Working range10″–24″
Pressing force on tools6 600 N
Max wheel width320 mm · 13″
Working pressure7 bar
Weight70 kg

Not a machine of its own — it stands beside a tyre changer and does the holding a second pair of hands would. A tapered roller keeps the first bead in the rim well, a large disc lifts the tyre to free the lower bead, and a rotating depressor and presser cone hold a stiff tyre down on the turntable. That is what makes run-flat, ultra-low-profile and alloy work manageable single-handed.
