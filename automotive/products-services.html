Services

Support beyond the equipment

Supplying the machine is the start. These services are quoted separately, so you take only what your workshop needs.

Technical Training

Spooring and balancing courses for workshop mechanics and sales counter staff, run at your premises.

Installation & Commissioning

Maintenance & Spare Parts

Service visits and genuine spare parts, backed by after-sales support from the team that installed the machine.

Two training courses, taught as theory and workshop practice:

For mechanics

Spooring & Balancing

30 hours · 12 theory, 18 practical · 10–15 per class

Suspension and steering, the wheel angles that govern how a car drives — toe, camber, caster — and wheel balance. Practice covers setting the angles, reading alignment data and optimising balance.

For sales counter staff

Spooring & Balancing

24 hours · 12 theory, 12 practical · 15–20 per class

The same fundamentals, angled at the customer conversation: why a vehicle needs alignment and balancing, how to diagnose the complaint behind the booking, and how to talk a customer through the alignment printout.

Courses run at your workshop — a meeting room for the theory, your service bay for the practice — with materials supplied. Taught by a trainer with 33 years in spooring and balancing instruction, an automotive engineering graduate and BNSP Master Assessor. Every participant receives a Hanindo Automotive certificate of training; national competency certification through LSP Global Otomotif is available on request.
