The training programme covers theory and practice, and is open to everyone:

Technical training

Spooring & Balancing

30 hours · 12 theory, 18 practical · 10–15 per class

Suspension and steering, the wheel angles that govern the direction a car travels — toe, camber, caster — and wheel balance. Practice covers setting the angles, reading alignment data and optimising balance.

Counter staff training

Spooring & Balancing

24 hours · 12 theory, 12 practical · 15–20 per class

The same fundamentals, angled at the customer conversation: why a vehicle needs alignment and balancing, how to diagnose the complaint behind the booking, and how to talk a customer through the alignment printout.

  • On-site training — Theory in a meeting room and hands-on practice in a service bay
  • Materials provided — All training materials included
  • Experienced trainer — Automotive engineering graduate and BNSP Master Assessor with 33 years of spooring and balancing instruction
  • Training certificate — PT. Hanindo Automotive certificate issued to every participant
  • National certification — LSP Global Otomotif competency certification available on request
