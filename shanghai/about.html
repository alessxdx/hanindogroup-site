About Us

Company overview

Where the CUSTOM
partnership started

Hanindo (Shanghai) International Co., Ltd. is the first of the Group’s three CUSTOM companies and the foundation for its regional CUSTOM business. Following the Group’s partnership with CUSTOM in 2005, the Shanghai office was established in 2007 as Hanindo’s first operation outside Indonesia, with Shenzhen following in 2009 to support the company’s growth in China.

Establishing a registered company in China, rather than operating through an agent, was a deliberate choice to maintain direct control over customer support, spare parts and technical service. This approach remains central to Hanindo’s commitment to providing reliable long-term support for every system delivered.
