About Us

Company overview

Four decades in
petroleum equipment

PT. Hanindo Citra has represented Gilbarco Veeder-Root since that first year — four decades of partnership, serving the same industry with the same principal.

Since 2012, it has also carried the group's oil and gas automation work, which transferred from PT. Hanindo Automation Solutions. This brings both the automation systems and the equipment they support under one company.

PT. Hanindo Citra also delivers the group's fire protection work through its Fire Fighting Department.

Retail forecourt equipment
The principal we distribute

Sole distributor for Indonesia.

Since 1987, PT. Hanindo Citra has been the sole Indonesian distributor for Gilbarco Veeder-Root — fuel dispensers, automatic tank gauging and submersible pumps, backed by local installation and after-sales service.
