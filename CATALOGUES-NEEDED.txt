HANINDO AUTOMOTIVE — PRODUCT BROCHURES NEEDED
==================================================
One PDF per model, with one exception: tyre inflation takes a single
Airtec series catalogue across all eight of its cards. A card shows its
"Download brochure" button once its file is in; until then it carries no
button at all, so nobody ever clicks through to a missing file.

Drop the PDF into /catalogues/ using the EXACT filename below and tell
me — the button is one line of HTML per card and I will add it. There is
no automatic pickup: a static site cannot check whether a file exists,
which is exactly why a card without a brochure shows nothing rather than
a dead link.

FILENAMES are the product's photo filename with a .pdf extension. That is
deliberate — hpa-b35.png and hpa-b35.pdf are the same machine, so there
is only ever one slug per model to remember, and a typo in either shows
up immediately next to its twin.

STATUS MARKERS, same as PHOTOS-NEEDED.txt:

  [DONE] / [COMPLETE]   the brochure is in and the button is live
  [NEEDED]              nothing there yet

NO PRICING, confirmed by the business: a brochure states what the
machine does, and a price comes only after the customer makes contact.
These pages are public and indexed by Google, so a PDF with prices in it
would be a public price list and would make "Request a quotation"
pointless. Check each file before it goes in.

Keep files under ~5 MB where you can. They are photo-heavy documents and
the button shows the size next to it, so a 30 MB file advertises itself
as a bad idea to anyone on mobile data.
