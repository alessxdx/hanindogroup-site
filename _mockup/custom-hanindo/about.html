About Us

Company overview

From a Shanghai office
to a network across Asia

CUSTOM Hanindo is the Hanindo Group’s CUSTOM business. It began with the Group’s partnership with CUSTOM in 2005, and in 2007 Hanindo (Shanghai) International Co., Ltd. opened in Shanghai as the Group’s first operation outside Indonesia, with a Shenzhen office following in 2009.

In 2012 the CUSTOM range came to Indonesia through PT. Hanindo Automation Solutions, one of the Group’s four founding companies, first established in 2002 for oil and gas automation. Two years later Gralessando (S) Pte. Ltd. opened in Singapore as the hub for Southeast Asia, followed by partnerships in Vietnam, Thailand and the Philippines and a Manila office in 2023.

In every market we chose to operate through our own registered company rather than an agent. That keeps customer support, spare parts and technical service in our own hands, and it remains central to how we support every system we deliver.
